Custom cover art — is it possible?

Two unknowns sit in front of this feature. This answers the one that doesn't need anybody to reconnect their account.

Can we read album artwork back off a canvas? Building a cover from four album images means drawing them and exporting the result. If Spotify's image CDN doesn't send CORS headers, the canvas is tainted the moment an image lands on it and the export throws — the picture is visible on screen and unreadable by the page.

The other unknown isn't testable here: uploading needs the ugc-image-upload scope, which every existing user would have to reconnect to grant. That's a product decision, not a technical one.
