DeepDive

You've liked the album version. You missed the single.

DeepDive reconciles an artist's catalogue against your Spotify library — finding the recordings you already love hiding under a different release, and everything by them you've never heard at all.

Open DeepDive

Free. Runs entirely in your browser. Takes about two minutes to set up.

Find what you missed

You liked a song off an album years ago. The same recording turned up later on an EP, and Spotify showed it to you like it was new. DeepDive reads an artist's whole catalogue against your Liked Songs and finds those near-misses — matching on the recording itself, not just the title.

Build the playlist

Everything by that artist you genuinely haven't heard, in album order or however you like it — length, ordering and naming all yours. Nothing touches your library until you press the button.

Sample what you barely know

Artists you've liked once or twice and never followed up on. Each one leads with the song you already know, then two you don't — an introduction rather than a pile of strangers.

Playlists from your own history

Your 2019. Albums that landed. Music you found twenty years late. One from every year you've been collecting. All built from what's already in your library, with nothing created until you confirm it.

Pins and suggestions

Pin an artist to come back to. Suggestions come half from what you've been playing and half from your own library, and each one tells you why it's there — "one song liked", "last added 2019".

Yours alone

No server, no account, no data collected — there's nowhere to collect it to. Everything happens in your browser, between you and Spotify. Undo what it did, and export your settings whenever you like.

Spotify requires every app to have its own credentials, so you'll create a free one on their developer dashboard. It's a form, and you only do it once.
